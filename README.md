# Random-scary-co-ordinates
**There are lots of strange place in the world. Google Maps just makes them easier to find.**
Welcome to my **Random-creepy-place-coordinate-collection**, an intercative collection of real-world places with questionable histories, unsettling legends and stories that make you wonder why humans are so determined to explore things.

## What's inside?
**Spooky Co-ordinates roulette:** Randomly discover weird places around the world. (Will expand to cover more.)
**Categorised Locations:** Urban Folklore, Unanswered Oddities, Abandoned Locations and Natural Mysteries.
**Mystery Index:** An unoffical rating of how questionable a place's history is. (NOT AN INCENTIVE TO ACTUALLY GO AND WALTZ AROUND THE PLACE.)
**Google Maps Integration:**Because just reading about strange locations is boring. We need coordinates as well, with Google Maps telling you to "turn left at the next roundabout, turn lef—"

## Built With
Html, CSS and Javascript(and caffeine. obvs.)

## DISCLAIMER
Not every mystery is the work of some scorned ghost lover. Some are folklore, historical accounts and some are simply humans being dramatic.
All locations are real though but not all stories are verified.
